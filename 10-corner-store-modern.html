Why we clean with water, not chemicals

Most of the industry still soaks clothes in PERC, a harsh solvent that is tough on fabric, tougher on the people handling it, and the reason your "clean" jacket smells like a chemistry set.

We went a different way, and we went first. Glenforest was Toronto's first wet cleaning specialist: precision washing in water and air with biodegradable detergents, on Miele certified machines tuned to each fabric. Colours come back brighter and wool comes back softer. Nothing comes home smelling like anything but clean.

It is gentler on your clothes and on the people pressing them all day. The block we have shared with you for sixty years gets a break too.
