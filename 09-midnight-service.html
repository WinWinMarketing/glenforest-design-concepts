Toronto's first wet cleaning specialist.

Conventional dry cleaning leans on PERC, a harsh solvent that lingers in fibre, on skin, and in the air. Glenforest chose water instead, and became the first cleaner in Toronto to specialize in professional wet cleaning.

Our Miele certified machines measure temperature, motion, and detergent to the degree and the gram, so wool keeps its shape, silk keeps its hand, and colour stays true. Nothing toxic ever touches your clothes.

It is slower, quieter work. We think your clothes, and the people who wear them, can tell.

What touches your clothes

  • Pure water, measured to the gramProgrammed for each fabric, each time
  • Biodegradable detergentsGentle on fibre, gentle on skin
  • Nothing elseNo PERC, no harsh chemicals, no residue
