Our care

Water, not solvents.

Most dry cleaning still leans on harsh solvents like PERC. Glenforest was the first cleaner in Toronto to replace them with professional wet cleaning: pure water, biodegradable detergents, and Miele machines that meter every degree and every gram.

The result is cleaning that is 100 percent toxic-free and noticeably kinder to fine fibres and to sensitive skin. Garments come back soft, bright, and free of that chemical finish, pressed to the standard we have kept for over 60 years.

  • No PERC, ever
  • Biodegradable detergents
  • Miele certified process
  • Safe for skin, silk, and wool
