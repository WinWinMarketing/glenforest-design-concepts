Our Care

Dry cleaning was never actually dry.

Conventional dry cleaning bathes your clothes in petroleum solvents, most often perchloroethylene, a chemical you would not knowingly bring near your skin. It cleans, after a fashion. It also dulls colour, stiffens fibre, and leaves its signature on everything it touches.

Wet cleaning is the older, wiser idea done with modern precision: pure water, biodegradable detergents, and Miele machines that measure temperature and movement to the degree. It is gentler on wool, truer to silk, and completely toxic free. No PERC, no harsh chemicals, no chemical smell hanging in the closet.

Wool comes back softer and silk keeps its colour: water and skill, not perchloroethylene.

Glenforest was the first house in Toronto to commit to it entirely. Sixty years at the pressing table teaches you the difference between cleaning a garment and caring for one.
