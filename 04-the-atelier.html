No. 03

We clean with water, not solvents

Conventional dry cleaning leans on PERC, a solvent that lingers in fabric long after the press. We took a different path decades ago and became Toronto's first wet cleaning specialist.

Every garment is cleaned in pure water with biodegradable detergent, on Miele certified equipment programmed for that fabric. Wool keeps its loft, silk its hand. Your clothes come home brighter, with no solvent residue or odour.

One hundred percent toxic free. No harsh chemicals. No PERC. Sixty years of proof.

Three generations of the Zachos family, one corner of Yonge Street.

Glenforest Cleaners has been family run for over sixty years at Yonge and Lawrence, caring for the wardrobes of the neighbourhood from the same shop at 3309 Yonge Street. The methods have been refined; the standard has never moved.
