Water, chosen over solvent.

Most of the industry still leans on PERC, a harsh solvent your clothes carry home with them. Glenforest went a different way decades ago, becoming Toronto's first wet cleaning specialist: pure water, controlled to the degree, matched to each fibre.

Done properly, wet cleaning is kinder than conventional dry cleaning in every way that matters. Wool keeps its softness, silk keeps its light, and colors stay true. Our Miele certified equipment and six decades of hands make sure of it.

Every garment is cleaned in water and detergent matched to its fibre; there is no PERC in the building.
