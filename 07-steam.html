Water is the gentler expert.

Conventional dry cleaning leans on PERC, a harsh solvent your skin can smell the moment you unzip the bag. Decades ago, the Zachos family chose a different path and became Toronto's first wet cleaning specialist.

Wet cleaning uses pure water, biodegradable detergents, and computer controlled Miele machines that treat each fabric on its own terms. It reaches deeper than solvent ever could, then lets go completely: no residue, no odour, no compromise.

The Miele cycle sets water temperature, drum motion, and drying moisture for each fabric, which is how wool, silk, and beaded pieces most cleaners refuse come through safely. Everything is back on your next route day.
