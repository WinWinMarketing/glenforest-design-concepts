No. 2 / The Water Method

Why water
wins.

Most dry cleaning is not dry. It is a bath in petroleum-derived solvent, most often PERC, a chemical you can smell on the collar. Glenforest was the first house in Toronto to walk away from it. We clean in water, with biodegradable detergents and computer-controlled Miele machines, and the result is not a compromise: colours come back brighter, whites come back whiter, and nothing comes home smelling like a laboratory.

It is gentler on silk and wool, and easier on skin that reacts to solvent residue.

No PERC · No harsh chemicals · Ever
  1. 01

    Read the garment

    Fibre, weave, dye, buttons, and the story of the stain. Nothing is cleaned before it is understood.

  2. 02

    Spot by hand

    At the board with brush and bone, working stains out of the cloth instead of blasting them through it.

  3. 03

    Clean in water

    Miele-certified machines, exact temperatures, biodegradable detergents. One hundred percent toxic-free, every load.

  4. 04

    Finish and press

    Dried gently, blocked to shape, and pressed until it hangs the way its maker intended.
